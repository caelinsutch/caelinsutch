Seven weeks without writing. The longest gap since I started this practice.

Lessons about consistency: it's easier to maintain than restart. Publishing standards can become excuses for not publishing at all. Sometimes you just need to ship something imperfect.

Work at the National Design Studio has been intense. We launched multiple government websites—Genesis Mission, Tech Force, and several others I can't discuss yet. Each one a small victory in the larger mission of making government digital services actually usable.

The biggest focus has been developing the American Design System. ADS will eventually replace USWDS, addressing a fundamental challenge: how do you consolidate dozens of diverse federal visual languages into something coherent without losing what makes each agency's work distinctive?

It's a design problem wrapped in a political problem wrapped in a technical problem. Progress is measured in meetings and alignment documents as much as in code.

Personal projects haven't stopped. Poppy continues to expand—my AI assistant now handles more of my daily coordination than I expected possible six months ago. Completed a Mac MCP for contacts and iMessage interaction, which feels like the kind of invisible infrastructure that compounds over time.

Published a Figma plugin template that a few people have found useful. Small contributions to the ecosystem.

Also acquired an Ableton Push 3. Music production as a creative outlet disconnected from screens and text. Working with LED art installations—light as a medium has always fascinated me.

The holidays approach. Time to slow down, reflect, prepare for what 2026 demands.
